Before you begin

A little about me, before us.

I'm an introvert, and sometimes I have a whole conversation planned in my head, only for the words to disappear when I actually need them. 😂 That's one reason I love texting: it gives me time to gather my thoughts.

After several situationships, this is my first actual relationship experience. I'm learning what it means to build something deeper with someone, and I want us to learn each other instead of expecting either of us to magically know everything.

This isn't a rulebook or a list of demands. It's a little window into who I am, what makes me feel loved, what I'm still learning, and the kind of connection I hope we can build together.
